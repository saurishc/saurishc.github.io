A function of a complex variable $f(z)$ is an ordered pair of two real functions, $u(x,y)$ and $v(x,y)$, each of two real variables $x=$Re $z$ and $y=$Im $z$. Thus,

$$
f(z)=u(x,y)+iv(x,y)
$$
