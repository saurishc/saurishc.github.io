I have been inactive in posting to my website for the last eight months. There are too many incomplete posts with lecture notes. With my students, whenever required, I have been sharing handwritten notes (most of my students are unaware of my website). However, I usually discourage students from relying just on the lecture notes. Instead, I encourage them to use lecture notes as a list of the topics which I cover and read the corresponding sections of the textbook or the reference material which I used to prepare my notes.

Now, I have a plan. Instead of rewriting my lecture notes for my website, from now on, I will upload the notes in pdf format and link them in my [Teaching Page](https://saurishc.github.io/teaching.html) as before. Occasionally, I will post some of my thoughts here.
